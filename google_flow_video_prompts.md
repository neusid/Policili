# 🎬 Google Flow — Video Ad Prompts
**"Tech-savvy Developer Portfolio Ad"**  
Duration: ~30 seconds | 5 scenes | Dark & Sleek Aesthetic

---

> **Cara pakai:** Copy masing-masing prompt ke Google Flow, generate per scene, lalu edit/gabungkan hasilnya di video editor favoritmu (CapCut, Premiere, dll).

---

## 🎬 Scene 1 — Opening Hook (0–5 detik)

**Tujuan:** Grab attention, kenalkan sosok  
**Durasi scene:** 5 detik

```
A young male tech professional in his late 20s sits at a sleek dark workstation, ambient neon blue and purple lighting illuminating his face. He wears a fitted casual dark hoodie, headphones around his neck. Three ultrawide monitors glow with Figma UI design screens and VS Code. He leans forward confidently, fingers hovering over a mechanical keyboard. The room is dark with subtle bokeh light particles floating in the air. Cinematic close-up shot, slowly pulling back to a wide-angle. Mood: focused, modern, premium. Photorealistic, 8K.
```

---

## 🎬 Scene 2 — Skill Showcase: UI/UX Design (5–12 detik)

**Tujuan:** Tunjukkan keahlian UI/UX & Figma  
**Durasi scene:** 7 detik

```
Close-up shot of a young male developer's hands working on a large monitor showing a beautiful Figma design prototype — colorful app UI wireframes and component libraries. On a second screen, a smooth prototype animation plays automatically. The background is dark studio with soft purple neon strip lights. The developer glances at the camera briefly with a slight confident smile, then refocuses. Camera slowly pans left-to-right across the dual-screen setup. Sleek, cinematic, photorealistic, 4K quality. Text overlay visible on screen: "UI/UX Design • Figma Prototyping".
```

---

## 🎬 Scene 3 — Skill Showcase: Mobile & Web Dev (12–20 detik)

**Tujuan:** Tunjukkan keahlian Flutter, React Native, dan Web Dev  
**Durasi scene:** 8 detik

```
A tech-savvy young male developer holds a smartphone in one hand showing a polished mobile app interface (Flutter design), while his other hand types on a laptop showing React code. The environment is a modern dark co-working studio with ambient LED lighting in cyan and blue tones. Multiple mockup devices (phone, tablet, laptop) float in a holographic-style display around him. The atmosphere is dynamic — soft light beams cutting through a dark room. Slow-motion cinematic sequence, 4K, photorealistic. Screen shows snippets of Flutter and React Native code side by side.
```

---

## 🎬 Scene 4 — Social Proof / Work Montage (20–26 detik)

**Tujuan:** Tampilkan hasil kerja / portfolio  
**Durasi scene:** 6 detik

```
A fast-cut montage sequence showing: (1) a sleek dark company profile website loading on a browser, (2) a mobile app UI scrolling smoothly on an iPhone mockup, (3) a Figma design screen with multiple artboards. Each shot is 1–2 seconds, sharp and high contrast. Overlaid text labels appear briefly: "Company Profile", "Mobile App", "UI Redesign". The color palette is dark navy with neon blue-purple accents throughout. Background ambient music energy rises. Cinematic, 4K, dynamic quick cuts.
```

---

## 🎬 Scene 5 — CTA Closing (26–30 detik)

**Tujuan:** Soft close, kesan mendalam, undang kolaborasi  
**Durasi scene:** 4 detik

```
The same young male developer leans back in his chair, crossing his arms with a calm, confident smile, looking directly into the camera. The background slowly defocuses into bokeh of neon blue and purple lights. Text fades in elegantly at the bottom of the frame in clean sans-serif typography: "Let's build something great together." Below it, a subtle animated underline appears beneath a portfolio URL. The lighting on his face is warm-cool split — professional and cinematic. Final shot holds for 3 seconds. Fade to black with text remaining on screen. 4K, photorealistic, cinematic grade.
```

---

## 🎨 Global Style Reference (pakai di semua scene)

Sertakan parameter ini secara konsisten di setiap prompt untuk menjaga visual coherence:

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| **Lighting** | Dark studio, neon accent: blue `#00B4FF` & purple `#9B59FF` |
| **Character** | Young male, late 20s, casual-smart, dark hoodie |
| **Mood** | Focused, confident, premium, modern |
| **Camera** | Cinematic — mix of close-up, pull-back, slow pan |
| **Quality** | Photorealistic, 4K–8K, cinematic color grading |
| **Pace** | Scene 1–3 slow & immersive, Scene 4 fast cuts, Scene 5 calm close |
